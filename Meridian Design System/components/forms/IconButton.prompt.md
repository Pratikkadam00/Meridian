Icon-only tappable target for toolbars, headers, and row affordances. Always supply `label`.

```jsx
<IconButton icon={icon('bell')} label="Reminders" variant="tonal" />
<IconButton icon={icon('more-horizontal')} label="More" />
```

Variants: `plain` · `tonal` (jade wash) · `filled` (jade) · `danger`. Min 44px target.
